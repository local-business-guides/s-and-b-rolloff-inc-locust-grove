What to have ready before you call

A few basic details help make the first conversation useful. Gather:

  • The site address and the best contact for the job.
  • Whether the need is for a project or recurring commercial service.
  • A plain description of the material and an estimate of how much there is.
  • Where a container could be placed, if you are asking about roll-off service.
  • Your target start date and any access or scheduling limitations.

You don’t need to guess at a container size or service plan. Share what you know and ask what other details the provider needs. If the work is at a managed property, check with the property manager about placement and access before arranging delivery.

Mistakes to avoid when arranging service

Don’t describe mixed or unusual material as general waste without asking what the provider can handle. Don’t assume a roll-off can be placed anywhere on a site, or that a truck can reach it without obstruction. Confirm the location, access, and service details in advance. If you’re considering a transfer station visit, check the weekday schedule and ask what to expect before loading up.
